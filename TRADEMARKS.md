**مجاز نیست:**

- عرضهٔ نسخهٔ تغییریافته با نام «پلتفرم حقوقی» یا «Legal Platform»، یا با لوگوی پروژه، طوری که نسخهٔ رسمی به نظر برسد.

**Allowed without asking:**

- Installing and running the official project under its name, for your own practice or your clients.
- Stating accurately that a modified version is "based on Legal Platform by Mohammad Ansari".
- Writing articles, tutorials or reviews that mention the project by name.

**Not allowed:**

- Distributing or hosting a modified version under the names "Legal Platform" or "پلتفرم حقوقی", or with the project logo, in a way that suggests it is the official project.
- Removing or hiding the author attribution from the footer, the About screen or the `NOTICE` file (license section 7(b)).
- Presenting yourself as the original author, or implying an endorsement or partnership that does not exist.

For a modified version, use your own name and logo and state in its About screen that it is based on this project. For anything else, contact [@ansariaiadmin](https://t.me/ansariaiadmin) on Telegram.
